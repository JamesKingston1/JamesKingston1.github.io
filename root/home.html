Jimmy Kingston

My name is James (Jimmy) Kingston. I am 30 years old and live in New Haven, Connecticut. This summer (2026) I received my PhD in physics from the University of California, Davis. I conducted my thesis research at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, which culminated in the construction and testing of the world's first percent-level xenon-doped dual phase argon time projection chamber (TPC). Such a detector technology could have unprecedented sensitivity limits to rare events such as $\mathcal{O}(1\text{--}3\ \mathrm{GeV})$ dark matter, as well as detection of neutrinos through the CoHerent Elastic Neutrino-Nucleus Scattering (CE$\nu$NS) channel.

After the completion of my PhD I began a postdoc position at Yale University.

My field of research is experimental particle physics: specifically, I am interested in the direct detection of dark matter. My current work focuses on xenon-doped liquid argon in TPCs, with the goal of exploring its applicability in dark matter and neutrino experiments.

My hobbies include fitness (with a focus in powerlifting, bodybuilding, and calisthenics), speedcubing, and listening to audiobooks.
I am also passionate about veganism. I want to show by example that it is a healthy and convenient lifestyle.
